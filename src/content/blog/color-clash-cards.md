## A World Where Color Matters

It is time to lift the curtain and look straight into the Game Design Document by Velocity Vale Studios for the TurboBlocks server!

In the near future, color is no longer cosmetic. It is energy, identity, currency, and a weapon. Our custom mode, **Color Clash**, represents sanctioned conflicts where competitors fight for dominance in chromatic arenas.

Each match will last between 8 to 15 minutes and host up to 16 players per arena.

![Color Clash Arena](https://placehold.co/800x400/1a0033/ff79c6?text=Color+Clash+Arena)

## Mechanics: Painting is Control

In Color Clash, painting surfaces is not decoration. It is absolute control.

Here is what you need to know about survival and elimination:
* Stepping onto an enemy-controlled surface triggers an elimination, visually dissolving the player into particles of their own color.
* There is no time to be bored — respawn is fast and re-entry into the arena is immediate.
* When a player paints continuously without being eliminated, they enter a flow state represented by a combo multiplier, which heavily boosts their scoring output.

![Combo Multiplier](https://placehold.co/800x400/0d0d2b/8be9fd?text=Combo+Multiplier+System)

## Arsenal and Power-ups

Forget traditional weapons. Tools in Color Clash are ways to project your presence.

* **Paint Brush**: Represents precision and intent.
* **Paint Cannon**: Built for reach and applying pressure.
* **Paint Roller**: The perfect gear for momentum and expansion.
* **Paint Grenades**: Used to create sudden moments of disruption.

Unstable bursts of condensed chromatic energy, known as power-ups, will also appear unpredictably. Players are meant to fight over them. You will find game-changers like Color Shields, Color Rockets, and the massive Neon Turbo Color.

![Arsenal Overview](https://placehold.co/800x400/1a001a/ff55cc?text=Arsenal+%26+Power-ups)

## Where and How Will We Play?

The environment is not static. As chromatic saturation increases, systems activate — platforms move and hazards emerge.

Each map has its own unique character:
* **Neon City**: Engineered dominance.
* **Volcanic Island**: Raw instability.
* **Frozen Realm**: Suppressed energy.
* **Rainbow Maze**: Experimental distortion.

![Maps Overview](https://placehold.co/800x400/001a1a/00ffcc?text=Chromatic+Regions)

You can test your skills in the individual dominance of **Free-for-All**, coordinated control in **Team Battle**, or dive into the unsanctioned overload of **Chaos Mode**.

Color Clash is not a side attraction — it is the foundation of TurboBlocks. Get ready for the spectacle!
